Most small businesses and freelancers don't have a CA on retainer. They check their bank balance and hope for the best. Financial problems compound in silence until they become crises.

What you get

Financial intelligence
built for your reality.

Core diagnostic
Financial risk scoring

A composite 0–100 score across three dimensions: profitability, liquidity, and leverage. Benchmarked against published data from HMRC (UK) and RBI (India) — not generic averages. You see your score, what drove it, and what it means for your business specifically.

Benchmarks: UK profit margin healthy ≥20% (HMRC Business Population Estimates). India ≥15% (RBI MSME Lending Data). Current ratio healthy ≥2.0 (Bank of England SME lending criteria). Debt-to-revenue high risk >60% (World Bank MSME Finance Gap Report).

Compliance
Audit readiness assessment

A structured checklist of the questions any regulatory review would ask first — VAT/GST status, record keeping, banking discipline, filing history. You get a readiness score plus specific gaps identified, in plain English.

UK framework references HMRC self-assessment requirements and Companies House compliance obligations. India framework references CGST Act 2017 GST thresholds and MCA ROC filing requirements.

Governance
ESG & governance layer

A lightweight governance assessment covering financial controls, transparency, compliance awareness, and basic sustainability indicators. Increasingly relevant as supply chain ESG requirements filter down to SMEs from larger clients and lenders.

Covers: financial reporting consistency, separation of business and personal finances, contract discipline, compliance awareness, and environmental impact awareness.

Pro feature
AI financial assistant

An AI assistant that knows your specific financial data and answers questions about it. Not generic financial advice — answers grounded in your actual numbers. Ask why your risk score is low, what a bank would think of your figures, or what to fix first before a funding application.

Powered by Anthropic Claude. Your financial data is passed as context — every answer is specific to your inputs, not generic. Clear disclaimer: Smit interprets and explains. For regulated tax or legal decisions, always confirm with a qualified professional.

Pro feature
Exportable PDF report

A professionally formatted report of your complete diagnostic — scores, ratios, flags, actions, and assessment. Formatted for real-world use: share with a bank manager, attach to a funding application, or bring to a CA meeting as a starting point.

Coming soon. The one-time report purchase is designed for businesses at a specific inflection point — funding, partnership, regulatory review — who want a credible document without a subscription.
