Core
Combined Smit Score

A single score combining financial risk (60% weight) and ESG governance (40% weight). The only tool that treats your financial health and sustainability position as a single integrated picture — because banks, investors, and large clients do the same.

Financial benchmarks: HMRC Business Population Estimates (UK) · RBI MSME Lending Data (India) · Bank of England SME lending criteria · World Bank MSME Finance Gap Report. ESG benchmarks: UK DBT SME ESG Guidance 2023 · SEBI BRSR Lite · ISO 26000.

Financial
Risk scoring + audit flags

Four financial ratios benchmarked against official data — profit margin, expense ratio, debt-to-revenue, current ratio. Each explained plainly with your specific figure vs the official threshold. Audit flags fire automatically when your numbers trigger regulatory concerns (VAT/GST thresholds, going concern indicators, liquidity risk).

VAT threshold source: HMRC Value Added Tax Act 1994. GST threshold: CGST Act 2017. Going concern: ISA 570. Liquidity: Bank of England SME criteria. Leverage: World Bank MSME Finance Gap Report.

ESG
ESG upgrade plan

3–5 concrete, low/zero-cost ESG actions tied directly to your specific numbers. Not generic sustainability advice — actions calculated from your expense ratio, cash position, and checklist gaps. Each action shows the estimated score improvement and practical steps. Increasingly required by banks, insurers, and large clients even for small suppliers.

Frameworks: UK Government DBT Small Business ESG Guidance 2023 · SEBI BRSR Lite Framework · ISO 26000 Section 6.6 (fair operating practices) · Financial Reporting Council corporate governance code.
