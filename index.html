ESG intelligence

ESG isn't optional anymore.
It's where contracts are won.

Smit's ESG layer is built from government and regulatory frameworks — not marketing checklists. Every action it generates is tied to your specific numbers, with a financial saving estimate included.

E
Environmental

Energy costs, waste, and supplier sustainability. For SMEs, environmental awareness is the entry-level requirement — and the actions that improve your E score typically reduce operational costs by 8–15% simultaneously.

UK DBT SME ESG Guidance 2023 · SEBI BRSR Lite Principle 6 · BSI BS 8001:2017
S
Social

Written contracts, fair dealings, and documented terms. These protect your payment rights and signal governance quality to banks and corporate buyers assessing their SME supply chains.

ISO 26000 Section 6.6 · UK Late Payment Act 1998 · MSME Development Act 2006
G
Governance

Regular financial reviews, dedicated business banking, and compliance awareness. The G pillar has the most direct impact on loan access, contract eligibility, and financial resilience under pressure.

FRC Corporate Governance Code · SEBI BRSR Lite Principle 1 · IoD India SME Guidelines

The business case for ESG — with numbers

Bank lending — UK high-street banks are beginning to incorporate ESG indicators into SME credit scoring. A 2023 British Business Bank report noted that 41% of lenders plan to factor sustainability into SME loan decisions by 2025.
Source: British Business Bank, Small Business Finance Markets Report 2023
Supply chain contracts — 65% of FTSE 100 companies now require ESG disclosures from Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers. SMEs that cannot provide them are increasingly disqualified from tender processes.
Source: PwC UK Supply Chain Survey 2023
Government procurement — UK public sector contracts above £5m now require supply chain ESG evidence. The Carbon Reduction Plan requirement has already affected SME subcontractor eligibility.
Source: UK Cabinet Office Procurement Policy Note 06/21
Cost savings — SMEs that actively track and manage their environmental footprint report average operational cost reductions of 8–15% within 12 months. Smit's ESG actions are selected specifically because they reduce costs.
Source: Carbon Trust SME Sustainability Report 2022
